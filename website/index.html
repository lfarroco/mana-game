Mana Battle — an autobattler played on a 3×3 board

Recruit a team, chain their reactions, and break the enemy crystal.

Mana Battle is an autobattler played on a 3×3 board. Units can't hurt each other, so all the damage lands on the enemy crystal and the fight becomes a race between two piles of cooldowns. Most fights come down to which reactions fire and where each unit is standing when they do.

The game

A small board that doesn't stay small.

A 3×3 board for each side, with your crystal in the middle of it. That's the whole battlefield, and every choice you make in a run shows up on it.

Everything runs on cooldowns

Units act on their own timers, not turns. Haste, slow and charge are the levers that decide how often your team gets to act, and a well-timed one beats a bigger number.

One unit acts, its whole row answers

Every ability can trigger reactions on other units. Row, column, neighbours or enemies: where a unit stands decides who reacts to it.

What you gain sticks

Encounter picks, recruits, orbs and promotions all carry over between fights. Enough of the good ones and a run reaches a Gold Victory; skip the wrong ones and you'll feel it in round 12.

Take it online

Fight teams other players have already built, climb the rating ladder, or stay in PvE and see how deep Infinite mode goes.
